02 — A History in Motion

Eras that shaped the art form

1917

The First Frames

Japan's earliest animators experiment with short silent films, laying the groundwork for a national art form.

1963

The Television Age

Limited animation techniques make weekly serialized storytelling possible, defining a distinctive visual rhythm.

1988

Cinematic Ambition

Feature films push detail, scale and emotion, earning global recognition for animation as serious cinema.

2010

The Digital Renaissance

Digital tools blend with hand-drawn tradition, expanding what a single frame can express.
