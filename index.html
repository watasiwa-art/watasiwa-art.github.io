What Is Anime?

Anime (アニメ) is Japanese hand-drawn and computer animation. The word itself is simply a Japanese abbreviation of the English "animation" — but over a century it has grown into a distinct medium with its own visual grammar, themes, and cultural weight.

Unlike Western animation, which long centered on family comedy and short-form gags, anime developed serialized storytelling, cinematic framing, and genres for every age — from children's adventures to political thrillers and existential science fiction. Limited animation techniques, born of postwar budget constraints, became a stylistic signature.

Anime became a unique art form because it treated animation as cinema, not just cartoon — a medium capable of serious drama, ambiguity, and beauty. That ambition, carried by artists like Tezuka, Miyazaki, and the studios that followed, turned a domestic industry into a global cultural force.
